
\section{API Endpoints}
\label{sec:api-endpoints}

\textcolor{red}{Review and complete this section}
The ezTrading platform exposes various functionalities through RESTful APIs, which follow standard HTTPS methods for ease of integration and encrypted using TLS. To ensure secure communication, all API access is restricted to authenticated users through OAuth~2.0 tokens. This security mechanism ensures that each API request is authorized, providing an extra layer of protection for sensitive operations. Table~\ref{tab:api_endpoints} summarizes all APIs used in the system.

\begin{table}[h]
\begin{adjustwidth}{-3cm}{-3cm}
\centering
\caption{API Endpoints for Bot Management}
\label{tab:api_endpoints}
\begin{tabular}{|l|l|}
\hline
\textbf{Action} & \textbf{API Endpoint} \\
\hline
\multicolumn{2}{|c|}{\textbf{User Bot Management}} \\
\hline
Retrieve a list of all active and inactive bots & \code{/user\_bots} \\
\hline
Modify the trading parameters of an existing bot & \code{/customize\_user\_bot} \\
\hline
Inactivate a bot & \code{/activate\_user\_bot} \\
\hline
Deactivate a bot & \code{/deactivate\_user\_bot} \\
\hline
Cancel subscription to an inactive bot & \code{/cancel\_user\_bot} \\
\hline
\multicolumn{2}{|c|}{\textbf{Bot Developement}} \\
\hline
Retrieve a list of all bots belonging to a developer & \code{/bot\_files} \\
\hline
Retrieve source file of a specific bot & \code{/load\_bot} \\
\hline
Modify an existing, unpublished bot & \code{/save\_bot} \\
\hline
Release a specific trading bot & \code{/publish} \\
\hline
Clone a bot to create a new bot with similar strategy & \code{/clone\_bot} \\
\hline
Delete an unpublished bot from the database & \code{/delete\_bot} \\
\hline
\end{tabular}
\end{adjustwidth}
\end{table}

\subsection{User Bot Management}
This set of APIs is specifically designed for bot management from the user's perspective. It enables users to keep track of their bots' states, whether they are active or inactive.

\subsubsection{/user\_bots}
\begin{tabular}{|l|p{10cm}|}
\hline
\textbf{Parameter} & \textbf{Description} \\
\hline
Arguments & None \\
\hline
Security Level & User Authenticated \\
\hline
Usage Example & \code{GET http://api.eztradinghub.com/user\_bots} \\
\hline
Example Response & \code{\{"bots": ["Bot1", "Bot2"]\}} \\
\hline
\end{tabular}
