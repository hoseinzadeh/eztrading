\section{Project Planning and Methodology}

This section outlines the approach and methodology employed in the development of the ezTrading platform. A well-structured plan ensures that the project proceeds efficiently and delivers the expected results within the defined timelines.

\subsection{Project Phases}

The development of the ezTrading platform is divided into distinct phases, each building upon the previous one. This phased approach allows for the gradual expansion of features and ensures a steady progression towards the final product. The project phases are as follows:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Minimum Viable Product (MVP)}: In this initial phase, the focus is on creating a functional platform targeting the US stock market and users. The core features, including the strategy builder, backtesting, user dashboard, and basic trading capabilities, are implemented for both web and mobile apps. This phase establishes the foundation of the platform.

  \item \textbf{Expanded Features and Data}: The second phase enhances the platform by adding more features and incorporating additional data sources. This includes expanding beyond the US stock market to include stock options, forex, and cryptocurrencies. Both the web and mobile apps receive updates to accommodate these new features.

  \item \textbf{Global Expansion}: The third phase takes the platform global, making it accessible to users worldwide. This involves localization, scalability enhancements, and integration with international financial markets. The platform becomes available to a broader audience.

  \item \textbf{Advanced Features}: Subsequent phases focus on adding advanced features, such as multiple account management, more robust charting tools, and advanced trading options. These updates cater to experienced traders and investors.

  \item \textbf{Continuous Improvement}: Beyond the initial phases, ongoing development and maintenance ensure that the platform remains up-to-date, secure, and user-friendly. Regular updates and optimizations are performed based on user feedback and market trends.

\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Development Methodology}

The development of ezTrading follows an agile methodology, allowing for flexibility and adaptability throughout the project's lifecycle. Agile principles prioritize collaboration, iterative development, and customer feedback. Key aspects of our development methodology include:

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Iterative Development}: Features and components are developed in short iterations, allowing for frequent testing and refinement.

  \item \textbf{Continuous Integration and Testing}: Code is continuously integrated into the main codebase, and automated testing ensures the stability and quality of the platform.

  \item \textbf{Regular Stakeholder Feedback}: User feedback is actively collected and used to drive development decisions, ensuring that the platform meets user needs.

  \item \textbf{Cross-Functional Teams}: Development teams are composed of individuals with diverse skills, promoting collaboration and efficient problem-solving.

  \item \textbf{Scalable Architecture}: The platform is designed with scalability in mind to accommodate future growth and increasing user demands.

\end{itemize}

This combination of project phases and agile development practices ensures that ezTrading evolves in a structured manner while remaining adaptable to changing market conditions and user requirements.
