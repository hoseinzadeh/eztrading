
\section{Introduction}

The financial market has seen a rapid increase in the use of automated trading systems. Individual traders and financial institutions alike are turning to trading bots to maximize efficiency and profits. However, the creation and management of trading bots often require specialized skills that many traders lack. The ezTrading project aims to bridge this gap by developing a user-friendly trading bot management system, initially targeting the U.S. stock market and later expanding globally.

This document further delves into the architectural design, technical specifications, and the developmental roadmap of the project.
